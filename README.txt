CLASSIC HOME SERVICES WEBSITE
==============================

Files:
- index.html: Website content, SEO metadata and JSON-LD Schema.org markup
- style.css: Responsive styling
- script.js: Mobile navigation and WhatsApp booking form

Preview:
Open index.html in a browser.

Before publishing:
1. In index.html, replace https://example.com/ in the canonical link with your final website URL.
2. Publish the files to your hosting provider (GitHub Pages is supported).
3. Verify the structured data with Google's Rich Results Test / Schema Markup Validator.
4. Keep your business phone, email, service area and services accurate.

WhatsApp booking:
The form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to +91 90601 59156. The visitor reviews and sends it themselves.

Note:
The schema uses the Purnea, Bihar service area because a full street address was not supplied.

Google Maps:
The supplied Google Maps short link is added in the contact section and footer.
